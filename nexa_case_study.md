# Nexa Search: A Premium Minimal Search Engine

**The Challenge**
Search engines today are often cluttered. They have too many ads and distracting links. I wanted to build something different. My goal was to create a search engine that feels calm and premium. It needed to give users exactly what they want without the noise.

**The Solution**
I built Nexa Search. It is a modern search platform focused on design and speed. I stripped away heavy colors and messy layouts. I chose a clean white background with deep teal accents. The interface is highly refined. Every animation feels smooth and intentional.

**Key Features**

**Intelligent Search Bar**
The search bar remembers your past queries. As you type, it drops down a neat list of matching history. You can click a small cross icon to easily clear any old searches. This keeps your experience private and organized.

**Premium Shopping Experience**
I added a dedicated shopping tab for products. When you search for items, you see a beautiful grid of cards. Hovering over a product card makes it lift off the page smoothly. It feels like browsing a luxury digital boutique.

**Local Map Results**
Searching for nearby places brings up a clean map pack. If you look for coffee shops, a sleek horizontal list appears. It shows photos, ratings, and operating hours instantly.

**Curated News Feed**
News feeds can easily become overwhelming. I built a smart loading system for the news tab. It shows the top ten stories first. If you want to read more, you just click a button to load the next batch.

**The Technology**
I built this project using React and Tailwind CSS. The application pulls live search data using a secure API. I focused heavily on frontend performance. The layouts use modern styling to ensure they look perfect on both phones and laptops.

**The Final Result**
Nexa Search proves that utility apps do not have to look boring. I focused on simple text and smooth physics. This helped me create a tool that feels expensive and unique. Users can find information quickly in a space that feels calm and focused.
